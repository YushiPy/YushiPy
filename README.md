### About me

I like turning mathematical ideas into working software, from geometric solvers and interactive visualizations to multiplayer games and AI-assisted tools.

- 🔬 Researching the **Touring Polygons Problem** at IME-USP, with FAPESP support.
- 📐 Built an exact C++ solver that was **112× faster on average** than the reference solver on the 558 benchmark instances from [Fekete et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2026.46).
- 🎓 Presented my research at **SIICUSP 33 and 34**. [Explore the interactive presentation →](https://yushipy.github.io/TouringPolygons/apps/siicusp34/)
- 🧑‍🏫 Former **Nonlinear Optimization** teaching assistant; **102 hours** of volunteer mathematics tutoring.
- 📍 São Paulo, Brazil.
