## Research

### [Touring Polygons Problem](https://github.com/YushiPy/TouringPolygons)

How do you find the shortest route that visits a collection of polygonal regions?

At IME-USP, I developed an exact, self-contained solver for the free-order problem with non-convex regions, combining computational geometry and mathematical optimization. On the 558 benchmark instances from [Fekete et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2026.46), my solver was faster in every case, with a **112× average speedup** over their reference solver.

I presented this research at **SIICUSP 33 and 34**, and built an [interactive site](https://yushipy.github.io/TouringPolygons/apps/siicusp34/) with problem instances, a challenge, a simulation, and results.
